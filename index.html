How it works

  1. Pin the engine. Serial, model code or model. It works out exactly which engine it is before it answers anything.
  2. Describe the fault. In your own words, the way you'd say it to the next bay.
  3. Get the next step. The test to do, the figure to check against, and the part to order, for that engine.

What it won't do

It won't guess the engine. It won't quote a figure until it knows which engine you've got. And it won't make something up to fill a gap. You'll get "I don't know" instead.
