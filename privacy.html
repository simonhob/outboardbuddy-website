Privacy Policy

Outboard Buddy — Last updated 27 May 2026

Outboard Buddy ("the App") is operated by Marine Imports UK Ltd ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Diagnostic conversations. Text you enter and responses generated during engine diagnostics are processed on our servers to provide the service. We may retain conversation history to improve the service.
  • Photos you attach. If you choose to attach photos from your camera or photo library, those images are uploaded to our servers solely to support your diagnostic session. We do not use them for advertising or share them with third parties.
  • Location. If you grant location permission, your approximate location is used only to find nearby marine dealers and services. It is not stored or shared.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect advertising identifiers, use third-party analytics SDKs, or track your activity across other apps or websites.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve engine diagnostic responses
  • To display relevant marine service locations near you
  • To maintain service reliability and diagnose technical issues

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your data with third parties except as required by law or to operate core infrastructure (e.g. cloud hosting providers under data processing agreements).

5. Data Retention

Conversation history is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@outboardbuddy.net.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at outboardbuddy.net/privacy.
