Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?
Use the Forgot Password option on the login screen. You'll receive a reset link by email within a few minutes.
How do I upgrade my account?
Visit your account settings within the app and tap Upgrade, or contact support@outboardbuddy.net and we'll help you directly.
How do I report incorrect engine information?
Use the contact form above and include the engine model, year, and a description of what appears to be incorrect. Our team reviews all reports.
How do I cancel a subscription?
You can cancel at any time by contacting us at support@outboardbuddy.net. Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period.
